# SECOND SUN — 75-second demo

Open the app at desktop width and click **Reset**. Read the displayed figures as you record.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–15 s | Opening budget and session counts | “The Hollow is an underground settlement with no sunlight. Crews share rooms with bright lighting, but the geothermal shortage leaves only 60 percent of the usual lighting budget. Tomorrow there are 18 requests for half-hour sessions. Only eight fit under this plan.” |
| 15–30 s | Select **Directorate**, then point to Levels 7 and 9 | “The fictional Directorate serves upper levels first. With the same rooms, shifts and energy limits, its plan gives the ventilation and utility crews no room time.” |
| 30–45 s | Select **SECOND SUN** and point to the same crews | “SECOND SUN gives the next available slot to a group that’s further behind on its requests. Here, both deep-level crews get two sessions. All 152 requesting residents get some access, while ten session requests still go unfilled.” |
| 45–60 s | Select **Simulate generator failure** | “Now the generator fails. The budget drops to 25 percent and the plan falls from eight sessions to three. The comparison shows which groups lose access under the same policy and room settings.” |
| 60–75 s | Click **View run sheet**, point to a booking, then click **Download CSV** | “The shift lead gets a start and end time, a room, the crew and the scheduled wattage. This is the plan we just simulated. It can be printed, downloaded or copied into the next shift’s handover.” |

All figures above describe the bundled fictional scenario. The run sheet updates whenever the budget, policy or room availability changes.
